# BOSS v9.5.33

**Release Date:** 2026-09-29

A preview of encrypted BossConsole window sharing arrives on macOS, letting your own signed-in
devices view and control a selected BossConsole window (#1768). This release also fixes a crash
with several macOS windows open, and keeps the download dialogs readable in the Liquid Glass
themes (#1776, #1766).

## Highlights

- **BossConsole window sharing preview** (#1768). On macOS 14 and later, share one BossConsole
  window, plus the dialogs it owns, with your other signed-in devices. Media is end-to-end
  encrypted, and viewers can watch from BossConsole or a browser
- **No more multi-window toolbar crashes on macOS** (#1776). Each window's native toolbar is now
  independent, so opening or closing a second window no longer aborts the app
- **Readable download dialogs in glass themes** (#1766). The Chromium download and Download Center
  dialogs stay opaque under Liquid Glass Light and Dark

## Features

- **sharing**: a new `Settings > BossConsole Sharing` page lists your windows, shows how many
  viewers are connected, and offers Take back control and Stop sharing. Sharing starts only from
  the native macOS Window menu, never from Settings or a remote viewer (#1768)
- **sharing**: exact window capture through ScreenCaptureKit. BOSS captures only the selected
  window and its owned dialogs, never the whole display, and works while the window is in the
  background or covered by another app (#1768)
- **sharing**: one remote device at a time can take control. Its input reaches only the shared
  window, with no system-wide key or mouse injection, and control is released on revoke, expiry,
  or when the window changes or closes (#1768)
- **sharing**: `Account Access` settings choose whether your devices join without approval and
  whether they can take control, both on by default. `Remote Connections` lists shared
  BossConsole instances on your account, to open in BossConsole or in a browser (#1768)
- **sharing**: sharing stops when the computer locks or sleeps, and does not resume by itself
  afterwards (#1768)

Everything inside a shared window is visible to its viewers, including sensitive tabs, and there
is no automatic redaction. Clipboard, file transfer, audio and guest access are not part of this
preview, and Windows and Linux are not yet supported.

## Bug Fixes

- **macOS**: native toolbars get a unique identifier per window, so AppKit no longer asks one
  window for another window's toolbar items and crashes (#1776)
- **theme**: the Chromium download and Download Center dialogs take their colors from the opaque
  base palette in Liquid Glass themes (#1766)
