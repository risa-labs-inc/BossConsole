# BOSS v9.5.41

**Release Date:** 2026-10-06

Organisation secrets now keep their ownership, management permissions and share attribution when
they reach plugins, both in process and in KERNEL mode (#554). The IPC contract moves to 1.5.0 to
carry the new fields.

## Highlights

- **Organisation secrets keep their permissions in plugins** (#554). Which organisation owns a
  secret, and whether you can manage it, now comes straight from the server through every plugin
  path, so plugins such as Secret Manager can show and enforce the right actions for shared
  organisation secrets
- **Share attribution is preserved** (#554). Secrets shared with an organisation keep the
  organisation they were shared with, so the share list says who has access rather than dropping
  that detail
- **Works in Microkernel Mode too** (#554). The same information is carried over IPC to
  out-of-process plugins, with the contract versioned as 1.5.0

## Bug Fixes

- **secrets**: organisation id, slug, ownership and management access are no longer lost when
  secrets are passed to in-process plugins (#554)
- **secrets**: organisation share targets and share attribution are carried through the in-process
  provider, the KERNEL-mode secret service bridge and the plugin-side IPC proxy (#554)

## Improvements

- **ipc**: additive `SecretService` fields for organisation ownership, management permission,
  share targets and organisation-share attribution, with the IPC contract bumped to 1.5.0. Older
  runtimes ignore the new fields; refreshed runtimes need the 1.5.0 `boss-ipc` artifact (#554)
- **secrets**: mapping tests cover every boundary the organisation fields cross (#554)
